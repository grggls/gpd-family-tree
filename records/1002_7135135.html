Full Source Citation. Collection description This database is an indexed collection of the draft cards from the Fourth Registration. The Fourth Registration, often referred to as the “old man’s registration”, was conducted on 27 April 1942. The records include name of registrant, age, birth date, birthplace, residence, employer information, and physical description.
